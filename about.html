About YourSTRL

One register for rental licenses, shared with the towns that need to see them.

Short-term rental rules are set town by town, and the licenses that prove compliance end up scattered across folders, inboxes, and renewal reminders. Managers can prove they are licensed, but it costs them hours that should go to the properties and to their own lives. Towns have to ask for the same documents again and again. We keep the licenses in one place and make them easy to share.

What we believe

Three commitments that decide what we build and what we refuse to.

The document is the point

The license a town issued is the proof. We keep the real thing on file, current and easy to find, instead of a status someone typed in a spreadsheet.

Work smarter, not harder

A renewal you chase every year for every unit is not hard work, it is repeat work. Managers should do it once, set it up right, and get those weeks back.

We are not the enforcer

We do not police anyone or decide who is compliant. We make it easier for operators to keep their licenses in order and for towns to see them.

Where we are today

We are early. This is what that means in practice.

Confirmed with your towns first

Tell us where you operate and we check what those towns accept first. No contract rests on a place we have not looked at.

Start small on purpose

Run it on part of a portfolio or a single district. Expand when it has earned the rest.

Built with the people using it

We work directly with managers and town staff, and their notes decide what gets built next.

You talk to the team building it

No handoff between the demo and the people who answer your questions later.

Who we work with

Property managers

Portfolios spread across several towns, each with its own permit process, where keeping up means hours that would be better spent on the properties themselves.

Towns and counties

Small teams who need to see a unit's license without chasing the operator for it every time.

Want to see it with your own licenses?

Thirty minutes, no preparation needed. Bring a few units and we will set them up with you on the call.
